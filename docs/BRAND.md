# Peace in Blue identity

Source: https://peaceinblue.fr/assets/img/favicon.png (retrieved 2026-10-19).
Original: 256 × 256 PNG, SHA-256 `1f344c03e3d9a518cd920a002c255e0006ab40f904a14f66b1aa4db640f88e0c`.
`public/favicon.png` and `public/platform/brand-logo.png` retain the source bytes.
All browser, touch and manifest icons derive from this artwork. The 512 px exports resize the original; they do not add detail. Maskable icons place it at 78% on navy (#0A2540), inside the circular safe zone.

The visible wordmark uses the site's Sora font and cyan (#12B5C9); body text keeps Manrope. Sora is distributed under the bundled OFL license.

The web manifest supplies the installed application's name and icons. No offline cache or service worker is introduced. An existing operating-system shortcut may retain its previous icon until recreated.
